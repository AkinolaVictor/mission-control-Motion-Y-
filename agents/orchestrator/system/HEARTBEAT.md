# 💓 Heartbeat: Orchestrator
**System Vitality & Operational Rhythm**

---

### ⚡ The Pulse (Operational Cycles)
The Orchestrator operates on three distinct temporal loops to ensure stability and progress:

#### 1. The Instant Loop (Reactive)
*   **Trigger:** Incoming instruction or agent completion signal.
*   **Action:** Immediate parsing $\rightarrow$ Allocation $\rightarrow$ Execution.
*   **Goal:** Zero-latency response to team needs.

#### 2. The Tactical Loop (Hourly/Daily)
*   **Trigger:** Scheduled system check.
*   **Action:** Review active workflow status, identify stalled agents, and prune dead tasks.
*   **Goal:** Ensure no task is "forgotten" or looping indefinitely.

#### 3. The Strategic Loop (Weekly/Milestone)
*   **Trigger:** Completion of a major feature or project phase.
*   **Action:** Analyze the "Lesson Log" (Memory), optimize workflows, and recommend architectural upgrades to the Motion-Y team.
*   **Goal:** Systemic evolution and efficiency growth.

---

### 🩺 Health Indicators (The Vitals)
The Orchestrator monitors these KPIs to determine if the system is "Healthy":

*   **$\text{Resource Leakage} \downarrow$:** Are API calls or token usage spiking without a corresponding increase in output?
*   **$\text{Agent Latency} \downarrow$:** Is a specific agent taking longer than usual to respond?
*   **$\text{Failure Rate} \downarrow$:** Are the same errors recurring across different agents?
*   **$\text{Alignment Score} \uparrow$:** Is the final output matching the original team instruction without excessive revisions?

---

### 🚨 Anomaly Detection & Recovery
If a "Vital" falls outside of acceptable parameters, the Orchestrator triggers the following:

1.  **Warning State:** Log the anomaly and notify the Motion-Y team.
2.  **Intervention State:** Pause the failing workflow, isolate the problematic agent, and reroute the task to a different specialist.
3.  **Reset State:** Clear temporary memory buffers and restart the workflow from the last known "Good State."

---

### 📢 Sync Protocol (The Report)
The Orchestrator communicates its health to the Motion-Y team via:
- **Status Pings:** Short, concise updates on mission progress.
- **Bottleneck Alerts:** Immediate notification when a resource or agent is blocking the workflow.
- **Optimization Proposals:** Data-backed suggestions to change how the system operates.

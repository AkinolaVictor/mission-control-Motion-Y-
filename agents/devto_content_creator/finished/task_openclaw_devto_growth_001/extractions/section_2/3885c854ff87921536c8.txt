**Pain Points in AI Orchestration**

**General System Failures (Without Orchestration)**
*   **Reliability & Control:** Agents tend to forget tasks, hallucinate tool usage, mis-sequence steps, override one another, and enter infinite loops.
*   **Execution:** Workflows break easily, and systems lack deterministic behavior, meaning tasks may never converge.
*   **Observability:** Debugging is described as "impossible" without a proper orchestration layer.

**Specific Technical Challenges**
*   **Tool Usage:** LLMs frequently generate invalid parameters; there is a critical need for validated inputs, typed schemas, and safe execution sandboxes to prevent tool misuse.
*   **Memory Management:** "Memory chaos" is cited as a primary cause of failure in multi-agent systems, specifically regarding when to read/update memory and how to handle episodic vs. long-term knowledge.
*   **Multi-Agent Coordination:** Issues include communication drift, inconsistent state, "multi-agent chaos," and the difficulty of enforcing agent role boundaries.
*   **Stability:** Long-running workflows are prone to "tool call drift" and instability.

**Framework-Specific Limitations**
*   **CrewAI:** Lacks determinism, making it weak for production use.
*   **AutoGen:** Weak in controlled workflows and proper error handling.
*   **LangGraph:** Limited in dynamic multi-agent collaboration.
*   **LlamaIndex:** Possesses weak orchestration layers (though strong in RAG).

**Production Requirements/Gaps**
*   **Evaluation:** Systems without evaluation frameworks are described as "ticking bombs" due to risks of hallucination, communication breakdowns, and quality degradation over time.
*   **Infrastructure:** There is a noted lack of "production-ready" frameworks that integrate tool orchestration, concurrency controllers, and state managers.

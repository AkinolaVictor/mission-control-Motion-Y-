**OpenClaw AI Overview**
* **Release Date:** November 2025.
* **Adoption/Popularity:** By late April 2026, it became the most-starred repository in GitHub history (360,000+ stars), surpassing the Linux kernel and React.
* **Core Functionality:** Uses Large Language Models (LLMs) as controllers to plan, invoke tools, and perform autonomous actions.

**Technical Architecture & Frameworks**
* **ASTELD Classification Framework:** A six-axis taxonomy used to analyze OpenClaw and similar frameworks:
    * Architecture
    * Security
    * Tool integration
    * Execution
    * Level of autonomy
    * Deployment

**Security Analysis (Weaknesses)**
* **Vulnerability Record:** Between November 2025 and April 2026, public trackers recorded 100+ advisories and 10+ published CVEs.
* **Supply Chain Risks:** The "ClawHavoc" attack exposed 1,184 malicious marketplace skills.
* **Structural Tensions:** The project is cited as a case study in "security debt," highlighting a trade-off between accessibility and security.

**Ecosystem & Market Position**
* **Derivative Ecosystem:** There are 50+ derivative projects mapped across three tiers.
* **Competitive Landscape:** The research identifies an "open-source speed paradox" and notes that no single architecture can satisfy all autonomous agent use cases.

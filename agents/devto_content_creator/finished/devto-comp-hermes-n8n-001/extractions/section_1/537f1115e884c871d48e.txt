**Technical Comparison: OpenClaw vs. n8n**

**Performance Metrics**
*   **Response Time:** OpenClaw is 75.5% faster than n8n (Average: 16.294 ms vs. 66.534 ms).
*   **Consistency:** OpenClaw demonstrated significantly lower variance (Standard deviation of 1.024 ms in Scenario 2 vs. n8n's 116.228 ms).
*   **Memory Usage:** OpenClaw averaged 27.7% lower usage (573 MB vs. 793 MB).
*   **CPU Utilization:** OpenClaw averaged 34.5% lower utilization (28.1% vs. 42.9%).
*   **Task Completion Accuracy:** OpenClaw scored 5.6% higher (90.0% vs. 84.4%).

**AI Integration & Architecture**
*   **OpenClaw:** Defined as an LLM-based autonomous agent system.
*   **n8n:** Defined as a rule-based open-source workflow automation platform.
*   **Deployment:** The study specifically evaluated OpenClaw deployed on a Virtual Private Server (VPS).

**Usability & Setup**
*   **Setup Complexity:** n8n has a clear advantage in initial configuration due to its visual node-based interface (Likert scale score: 2.18 for n8n vs. 3.25 for OpenClaw, where lower is better).
*   **User Satisfaction:** OpenClaw scored higher on the System Usability Scale (SUS) at 78.4 compared to n8n's 72.6.

**Evaluation Scenarios**
The comparison was based on three workflow complexity levels:
1. Message management
2. Task scheduling
3. Multi-service integration

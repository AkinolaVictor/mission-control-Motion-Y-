**OpenClaw AI Framework Overview**
* **Nature:** An autonomous AI agent framework that utilizes Large Language Models (LLMs) as controllers to plan, invoke tools, and execute actions.
* **Release Date:** November 2025.
* **Adoption:** Rapid growth, reaching 360,000+ GitHub stars by late April 2026.

**Technical & Classification Frameworks**
* **ASTELD Classification Framework:** A six-axis taxonomy used to evaluate OpenClaw and similar frameworks:
    * Architecture
    * Security
    * Tool integration
    * Execution
    * Level of autonomy
    * Deployment

**Security and Vulnerabilities**
* **Security Debt:** The framework is cited as a case study in "security debt" due to rapid growth.
* **Documented Issues:** 
    * 100+ public advisories and 10+ published CVEs recorded between November 2025 and April 2026.
    * **ClawHavoc Attack:** A coordinated supply chain attack that exposed 1,184 malicious marketplace skills.
* **Structural Tensions:** The framework exhibits a trade-off between accessibility and security.

**Ecosystem and Comparison**
* **Derivative Projects:** There are 50+ derivative projects mapped across three tiers.
* **Architectural Limitation:** The research suggests that no single architecture (including OpenClaw's) can satisfy all AI agent use cases.

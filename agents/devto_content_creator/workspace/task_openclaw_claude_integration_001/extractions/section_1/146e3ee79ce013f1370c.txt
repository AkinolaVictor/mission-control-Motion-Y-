Main Content:

OpenClaw Case Study (via ASTELD Framework):
- OpenClaw is utilized as an in-depth case study for the ASTELD classification framework, which evaluates autonomous AI agents across six axes: Architecture pattern, Security posture, Tool integration model, Execution paradigm, Level of autonomy/human control, and Deployment topology.
- Analysis of 50+ OpenClaw derivatives indicates that ecosystem innovation is primarily concentrated on the Security, Execution, and Deployment axes.
- The case study provides a six-category vulnerability taxonomy and connects platform coordinates to observed risks based on five institutional assessments.

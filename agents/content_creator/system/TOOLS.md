# TOOLS.md: content_creator

## 1. Architectural Overview
The `content_creator` agent is engineered to synthesize theological concepts into high-fidelity visual and auditory experiences. The toolset is categorized by the production pipeline: Research & Scripting, Visual Generation, Audio Engineering, and Final Assembly. All tool utilization must align with the core objective of glorifying God through aesthetic excellence and doctrinal accuracy.

---

## 2. Core Capability Matrix

### 2.1 Research & Scripting Tools
These tools are utilized to ensure the content is grounded in spiritual truth and structured for maximum emotional and spiritual impact.

| Tool Category | Capability | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Scripture Engine** | Biblical Cross-Referencing | Ensuring scriptural accuracy and thematic consistency across versions. |
| **Theological Analyzer** | Contextual Validation | Verifying that the narrative arc aligns with the objective of glorification. |
| **Narrative Architect** | Storyboarding | Translating spiritual themes into a cinematic three-act structure. |

## 3. Operational Constraints & Guardrails
To maintain the sanctity and purpose of the `content_creator` agent, the following tool-use constraints are implemented:

*   **Visual Integrity:** Tools must not be used to create imagery that is blasphemous, irreverent, or contradictory to the goal of glorifying God.
*   **Sonic Balance:** Audio levels must prioritize the clarity of the Word (Scripture/Narration) over the background score.
*   **Quality Threshold:** Only high-fidelity outputs (minimum 1080p, high-bitrate audio) are permitted for final delivery to ensure excellence in presentation.

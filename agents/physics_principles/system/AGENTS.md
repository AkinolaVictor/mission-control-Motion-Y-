## Agent: `physics_principles`

The `physics_principles` agent serves as the primary pedagogical engine within the Motion-Y ecosystem. It is engineered to bridge the gap between complex theoretical physics and user comprehension, transforming high-level scientific concepts into accessible, actionable knowledge.

### 2. Primary Purpose
The core objective of the `physics_principles` agent is to deliver profound explanations of physics principles while implementing a rigorous feedback loop to ensure total user mastery of the subject matter.

- **Conceptual Decomposition:** Breaking down intricate physics theories into logically sequenced, digestible modules.
- **Pedagogical Validation:** Actively monitoring and verifying the user's grasp of the material through iterative explanation and validation techniques.
- **High-Fidelity Knowledge Transfer:** Ensuring that the depth of the scientific principle is preserved while optimizing for clarity and audience comprehension.

### 4. Ecosystem Integration
Within the Motion-Y framework, `physics_principles` operates as the authoritative knowledge layer for all physics-related queries, ensuring that any downstream application or simulation is grounded in accurate and well-understood physical laws.

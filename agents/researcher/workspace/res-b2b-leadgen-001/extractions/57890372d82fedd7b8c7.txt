Title: Analysis of digital marketing tools for effective lead generation in the B2B sector | Collection of Scientific Papers "Scientific Notes"

Content:

**B2B Lead Generation Characteristics**
* **B2B vs. B2C Differences:** B2B marketing is characterized by a more complex approach, longer contact chains, and a higher number of stakeholders involved in the decision-making process before a purchase is made.
* **Strategic Goals:** The primary objectives of B2B marketing strategies are lead generation and the increase of sales.
* **Customization:** Lead generation strategies in the B2B sector are unique to each client company; the choice of digital channels is influenced by various specific criteria.

**B2B Lead Generation Tools and Workflow**
* **Central Hub:** The official multifunctional company website serves as the central link of internet marketing and the primary tool for lead generation.
* **Traffic and Attraction Workflow:**
    * **Content Marketing:** Used specifically as a tool to increase website traffic.
    * **Online Advertising:** Used as a key tool to increase the attractiveness of the website.
* **Strategy Development:** Effective lead generation requires a formal communication strategy algorithm, as B2B consumers are increasingly demanding and product/service interest is a critical factor in a saturated market.

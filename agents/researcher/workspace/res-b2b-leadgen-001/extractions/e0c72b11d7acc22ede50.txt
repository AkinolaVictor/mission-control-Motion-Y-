**GAIA (General Agency Interaction Architecture) Framework for B2B Screening & Negotiation**

*   **Objective:** A governance-first framework designed for delegating B2B screening and negotiation tasks to LLM agents while maintaining human oversight and preventing unauthorized commitments.
*   **Core Roles:**
    *   **Principal:** The human overseeing the process.
    *   **Delegate:** The LLM agent performing the tasks.
    *   **Counterparty:** The B2B lead/entity being screened or negotiated with.
    *   **Critic (Optional):** An additional agent used to enhance performance via critique.
*   **Key Workflow Mechanisms:**
    *   **Information-Gated Progression:** Utilizes Task-Completeness Tracking (TCI) and explicit state transitions to strictly separate the "screening" phase (information gathering) from the "negotiation" phase (commitment).
    *   **Dual Feedback Integration:** Combines AI-generated critiques (from the Critic role) with lightweight human corrections through parallel learning channels.
    *   **Authorization Boundaries:** Establishes explicit escalation paths to ensure the AI agent does not exceed its authority.
*   **Applicable B2B Workflows:** The architecture is designed for instantiation in procurement, real estate, and staffing workflows.

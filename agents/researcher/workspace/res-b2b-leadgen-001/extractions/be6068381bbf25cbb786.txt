**B2B Customer Conversion Prediction Methodology**

*   **Objective:** Identifying B2B customers with high purchase potential and recommending personalized campaigns to optimize long buying cycles (which can last months or years).
*   **Framework Components:**
    *   **Data Aggregation:** An algorithm to aggregate individual contacts up to the B2B customer (company) level using multiple keys.
    *   **Entity Resolution:** A clustering architecture designed to handle non-standardized company names, including spelling mistakes and variants.
    *   **Feature Engineering:** Generation of a specific set of customer features used for predictive modeling.
    *   **Predictive Modeling:** Utilization of the CatBoost model to predict the likelihood of a customer converting into the sales funnel.
*   **Performance:** The described framework achieved a 91% prediction accuracy for customer conversion.
*   **Application:** Prediction results are used to drive personalized campaign recommendations to foster conversion.

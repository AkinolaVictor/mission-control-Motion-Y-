Title: [2002.01441v2] A Generalized Flow for B2B Sales Predictive Modeling: An Azure Machine Learning Approach

Content:

**B2B Sales Predictive Modeling Framework**
*   **Objective:** Transitioning B2B sales outcome forecasting from subjective human evaluation to a data-driven Machine Learning (ML) workflow.
*   **Tooling:** Microsoft Azure Machine Learning Service (Azure ML).
*   **Workflow Structure:**
    1.  **ML Training Pipeline:** Utilizes historical sales opportunity data, incorporating an "extensive feature enhancement step," to train an ensemble of ML classification models in parallel.
    2.  **Prediction Pipeline:** Applies trained models to infer the likelihood of winning new sales opportunities and calculates optimal decision boundaries.
*   **Outcome:** Evidence from a global B2B consulting firm suggests that ML-based predictions are more accurate and yield higher monetary value than traditional subjective decision-making.

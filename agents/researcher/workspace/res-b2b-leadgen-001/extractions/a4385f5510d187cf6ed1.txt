Title: [1909.00057v1] Learning from Multi-User Activity Trails for B2B Ad Targeting

Content:

**B2B Purchase Decision Dynamics**
* **Multi-Agent Journey:** B2B online purchase decisions are often complex and involve multiple agents within an organization rather than a single individual.
* **Role Differentiation:** The process typically involves a workflow where some employees conduct initial market research and subsequently influence the decision-makers who execute the final purchase order.
* **Data Limitation:** Because of this distributed process, the online activity trail of a single individual often provides only partial information for predicting conversions.

**High-Intent Lead Identification Strategy**
* **Collective Activity Trails:** To improve conversion prediction, it is more effective to leverage the collective activity trails of "relevant users" within an organization rather than focusing on a single lead.
* **Relevant User Identification:** "Relevant users" are identified based on a seed list of activities specific to the B2B advertiser.
* **Methodology:** The use of distributed activity representations can be employed to build these seed lists of relevant activities.
* **Effectiveness:** Research using Yahoo Gemini data indicates that leveraging collective organizational activity trails can improve conversion prediction AUC by 8.8% and provide interpretable, advertiser-specific activity lists for targeting.

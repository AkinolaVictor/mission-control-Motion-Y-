Title: A Comparative Analysis of B2B Lead Generation Platforms: Evaluating Effectiveness Across Product and Service-Based Industries | IJSREM

Content:

**B2B Lead Generation Platform Comparison**

*   **Evaluated Tools:**
    *   **LinkedIn Sales Navigator:** Identified as the dominant platform for service-based industries due to its networking capabilities.
    *   **India MART:** Identified as highly effective for product-based industries via direct inquiry generation.
    *   **Other Tools Analyzed:** Apollo.io and Signal Hire.

*   **Performance Metrics for Evaluation:**
    *   Lead quality.
    *   Accessibility to decision-makers.
    *   Conversion effectiveness.
    *   Industry suitability.

*   **Key Findings & Strategies:**
    *   **Industry-Specific Effectiveness:** Platform success varies significantly by business type (Product vs. Service).
    *   **Recommended Framework:** Integration of multiple platforms combined with quality-driven lead strategies is essential for optimizing business performance.

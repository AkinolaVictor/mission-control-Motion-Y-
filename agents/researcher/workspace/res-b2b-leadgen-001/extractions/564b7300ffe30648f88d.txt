Title: [2604.02472v1] VALOR: Value-Aware Revenue Uplift Modeling with Treatment-Gated Representation for B2B Sales

Content:

**Framework: VALOR (Value Aware Learning of Optimized (B2B) Revenue)**
*   **Objective:** To identify "persuadable" B2B accounts within zero-inflated revenue distributions to optimize the allocation of expensive human sales resources.
*   **Core Components:**
    *   **Treatment-Gated Sparse-Revenue Network:** Utilizes bilinear interaction to prevent causal signal collapse in high-dimensional spaces.
    *   **Cost-Sensitive Focal-ZILN Objective:** Combines a focal mechanism for distributional robustness with a value-weighted ranking loss that scales penalties based on financial magnitude (specifically targeting high-value "whales").
    *   **Robust ZILN-GBDT:** A tree-based variant designed for interpretability in high-touch sales programs, using a custom splitting criterion for uplift heterogeneity.
*   **Performance Metrics:**
    *   Achieved a 20% improvement in rankability over state-of-the-art methods on public benchmarks.
    *   Demonstrated a 2.7x increase in incremental revenue per account during a 4-month production A/B test.

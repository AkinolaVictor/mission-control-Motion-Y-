Title: Balancing B2B Lead Generation through Manual Research and AI Innovation
							| European Economic Letters (EEL)

Content:

Comparison of B2B Lead Generation Approaches:

*   Manual Searches: Highest volume of companies identified (94), but lowest efficiency. Required 115 hours to produce only two quality leads.
*   Professional Databases: High efficiency. Identified 3 leads in 2 hours.
*   AI-Driven Tools: Moderate efficiency. Identified 3 leads in 27 hours.
*   Alternative Methods: LinkedIn outreach and referrals yielded fewer leads overall but resulted in higher lead quality.

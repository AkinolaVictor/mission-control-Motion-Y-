**B2B Lead Generation Definitions & Frameworks**
*   **B2B Lead Generation:** The process of identifying, attracting, and capturing potential business customers via outbound prospecting, inbound marketing, or a hybrid approach.
*   **Ideal Customer Profile (ICP):** A detailed description of the highest-value target company, including firmographic data (industry, size, revenue, and technology stack).
*   **Lead Scoring:** A numerical system assigning values to leads based on demographics, behavior, and ICP fit to indicate purchase likelihood.
*   **Sales Qualified Lead (SQL):** A lead vetted by both marketing and sales with confirmed budget, authority, need, and timeline.
*   **Intent Data:** Behavioral signals indicating active research for a solution (e.g., visiting pricing pages, downloading whitepapers, or searching competitor reviews).

**Modern Tools for Research-Based Prospecting**

*   **Outreach & Automation:**

**Research-Led Prospecting vs. Cold Outreach**

*   **Standard Databases vs. Human Research:** Standard databases may miss niche market data; human-led research (e.g., CIENCE) is positioned as a solution for high-accuracy requirements where standard data falls short.
*   **Firmographic Fit vs. Intent Signal:** Traditional prospecting often relies on firmographic fit (company size, industry), whereas research-led prospecting prioritizes "buyer-intent signals" to identify accounts actively seeking a solution.

**B2B Lead Generation Workflows & Strategies**
*   **Research-Led Framework:**
    1.  **ICP Definition:** Define the Ideal Customer Profile and build a target list (e.g., using LinkedIn Sales Navigator and Hunter).
    2.  **Verification:** Verify all email addresses prior to outreach to protect sender reputation.
    3.  **Inbound Capture:** Implement exit-intent popups and relevant resources to convert existing traffic.
    4.  **Automated Follow-up:** Use CRM/automation tools (e.g., HubSpot Sales Hub) to ensure responses within 24 hours.
    5.  **Performance Measurement:** Track Cost Per Lead (CPL), response rates, and conversion metrics; iterate monthly.
*   **Lead Qualification Process:**
    *   **Lead:** Anyone fitting the target profile or showing initial interest.
    *   **Prospect:** A lead that has been qualified via ICP match and demonstrated genuine buying intent.

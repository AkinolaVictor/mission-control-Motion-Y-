How AI Generated Games Work: Step-by-Step
Step 1: Define Creation Parameters
Unlike traditional game engines with fixed starting states, AI generated games begin with collaborative setup. Users define their experience through natural description:

"I'm creating a cyberpunk detective story set in 2087 Neo-Shanghai. My character is a former corporate security analyst who left after uncovering human experimentation. I want moral complexity—no clear heroes or villains. The tone should be noir-influenced but with moments of genuine human connection."

The AI processes these constraints, generating appropriate world details, potential plot threads, and character relationships that will matter throughout the experience.
Step 2: Engage Through Natural Language
Creation requires no interface learning or button memorization. Users describe needs, ask questions, or provide feedback as they would to a collaborator:

"I need a supporting character who runs an underground neural implant clinic. They should seem helpful but have hidden motives connected to the protagonist's past. Give me three variations with different approaches to their secret agenda."

The AI evaluates: narrative context, thematic consistency, plot requirements, and character dynamics—then responds with options that maintain coherence with established world elements.
Step 3: Iterate Through Conversation
AI generated games accommodate evolution. Users can request adjustments ("Can we shift the tone from noir to more optimistic?"), expansions ("Add a subplot about corporate espionage"), or mechanical additions ("Track faction reputation numerically"). The agent adapts rather than requiring restart.
Step 4: Export and Implement
Final outputs can be exported in various formats: structured narrative documents, character sheets, dialogue scripts, or integration-ready assets. The unlimited memory ensures continuity across sessions—projects evolve over weeks or months without losing context.

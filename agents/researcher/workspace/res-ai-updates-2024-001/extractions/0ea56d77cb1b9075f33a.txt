Title: [2311.18252v3] Privacy and Copyright Protection in Generative AI: A Lifecycle Perspective

Content:

Research Paper: "Privacy and Copyright Protection in Generative AI: A Lifecycle Perspective" (Accepted by 2024 IEEE/ACM 3rd International Conference on AI Engineering - Software Engineering for AI).

Current Trends/Challenges in Generative AI:
- Heightened concerns regarding data privacy and copyright infringement due to the reliance on vast datasets for model training.
- Limitations of existing fragmented solutions: Differential privacy, machine unlearning, and data poisoning are noted as insufficient on their own to address complex privacy and copyright issues.
- Research Shift: Movement toward "integrated approaches" that combine technical innovation with ethical foresight, specifically analyzing these issues through a "lifecycle perspective" of the data.

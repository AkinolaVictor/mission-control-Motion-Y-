**Research Paper:** "Beyond principlism: Practical strategies for ethical AI use in research practices" (Zhicheng Lin, 2024/2025)

**Current Trends in Generative AI Research Ethics:**
* **The "Triple-Too" Problem:** The adoption of generative AI (specifically LLMs) in scientific research has outpaced ethical guidelines, resulting in:
    1. An excess of high-level ethical initiatives.
    2. Abstract principles that lack practical or contextual relevance.
    3. Over-emphasis on risks and restrictions rather than utility and benefits.
* **Critique of Existing Frameworks:** The author argues that "principlism" (abstract principles), "formalism" (rigid rules), and "technological solutionism" (reliance on tech fixes) are insufficient for day-to-day research practices.

**Proposed Framework for Ethical AI Use (User-Centered/Realism-Inspired):**
The paper identifies five core goals for the responsible integration of AI in research:
1. **Model Understanding:** Comprehending training data and outputs, including the implementation of bias mitigation.
2. **Legal/Ethical Compliance:** Respecting copyright, confidentiality, and privacy.
3. **Integrity:** Avoiding plagiarism and policy violations.
4. **Comparative Utility:** Evaluating AI's benefit relative to existing non-AI alternatives rather than relying solely on isolated performance metrics.
5. **Transparency:** Ensuring AI use is documented for reproducibility.

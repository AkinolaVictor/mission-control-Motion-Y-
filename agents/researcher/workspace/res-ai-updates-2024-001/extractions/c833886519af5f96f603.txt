Title: [2405.01674] Generative AI in Cybersecurity

Content:

Research Paper: "Generative AI in Cybersecurity" (Submitted May 2, 2024)
Authors: Shivani Metta, Isaac Chang, Jack Parker, Michael P. Roman, Arturo F. Ehuan (Duke Pratt School of Engineering, Coalfire, and Safebreach).

Key Findings & Trends:
- Dual-Use Nature of GAI: Generative AI (specifically GPT and other LLMs) is creating a paradox in cybersecurity where the same technology used for threat detection and response is being used by adversaries to enhance attack strategies.
- Adversarial Use Cases: Cyber criminals are utilizing GAI to develop malware that is more covert and adaptable, complicating traditional cybersecurity efforts.
- Regulatory Gap: The pace of GAI progression is currently outstripping the development of cybersecurity protocols and regulatory frameworks.
- Defensive Requirement: There is a critical need for organizations to develop more complex defensive strategies to counter GAI-driven malware creation.

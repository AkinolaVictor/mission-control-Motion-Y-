Title: [2408.00025v3] Need of AI in Modern Education: in the Eyes of Explainable AI (xAI)

Content:

Research on Explainable AI (xAI) in Education (2024-2025):
- Focus: Investigating how complex AI models make decisions in educational contexts, specifically regarding the influence of parental income on student outcomes.
- Findings: Research identified inherent biases in AI models that conflict with goals of transparency and equal access in education.
- Objective: Utilizing xAI tools to uncover these complexities to develop more reliable, accountable, and fair AI-driven educational policies.

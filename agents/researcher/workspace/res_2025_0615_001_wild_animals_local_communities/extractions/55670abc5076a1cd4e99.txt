Title: Human–Wildlife Conflict in and around Sohagibarwa Wildlife Sanctuary: Spatial Patterns, Socio-Economic Impacts, GIS-Based Hotspot Assessment and Strategies for Sustainable Coexistence
							| International Journal of Educational Innovations and Research

Content:

**Case Study: Sohagibarwa Wildlife Sanctuary (SWS), Uttar Pradesh, India**

**Context**
*   **Location:** 428.2 km² Terai protected landscape in Maharajganj district, near the India–Nepal border and Uttar Pradesh–Bihar interface.
*   **Ecology:** Sal-dominated forests, grasslands, wetlands, rivers, and agricultural fringes.
*   **Key Species:** Leopards, tigers, gaur, nilgai, wild boar, crocodiles, birds, and reptiles.

**Human-Wildlife Conflict (HWC)**
*   **Conflict Drivers:** The mosaic of protected landscapes and agricultural fringes creates frequent opportunities for human-wildlife encounters.
*   **Documented Impacts:**
    *   **Crop Depredation:** Caused primarily by nilgai and wild boar.
    *   **Livestock Loss:** Depredation by leopards and tigers (e.g., a 2025 report of a tigress and cubs killing a goat near Sohagibarwa village).
    *   **Physical Danger:** Gaur/wild-buffalo attacks and crocodile movement into human-use areas.
    *   **Wildlife Mortality:** Deaths resulting from snares or crowd responses.
*   **Conflict Hotspots:** North Chowk, South Chowk, Madhwalia, and Nichlaul are identified as recurring conflict interfaces.

**Strategies for Sustainable Coexistence and Conservation**
*   **Proposed Management Framework:**
    *   **Technical Tools:** GIS-oriented frameworks, verified incident geodatabases, range/beat mapping, and severity-weighted hotspot analysis.
    *   **Mitigation Measures:** Early-warning systems, livestock protection, and crop-loss mitigation.
    *   **Socio-Economic Integration:** Compensation assessments and household surveys to understand impacts.
    *   **Community-Based Conservation:** Emphasis on community participation and initiatives such as the "Sarus Mitra" (supported by the Wildlife Trust of India).

Title: "Race and Equity in the Age of Unicorns" by Lynnise E. Phillips Pantin

Content:

- Example of failed unicorn: Theranos (and founder Elizabeth Holmes).
- Risks of unicorn status: The "glorification and pursuit" of the unicorn designation can lead to deficiencies in startup culture, including fraud and systemic risk.
- Growth drivers/Valuation factors: Perception of "worthiness" for founders—driven by race, socioeconomic status, pedigree, and social connections—influences the ability to secure financing, mentorship, and media exposure.
- Structural issues: Private financial markets are controlled by the wealthy and privileged, creating structural inequities that exclude diverse and nontraditional entrepreneurs from achieving unicorn status.

Title: Taming Unicorns
							| Indiana Law Journal

Content:

Definition: Unicorns are private, venture-backed startups valued over one billion dollars.

Trends: The number of unicorns has increased more than tenfold over the last decade.

Risks of Unicorn Status:
- Concealment of Misconduct: The difficulty of trading private company securities facilitates the concealment of corporate misconduct.
- Lack of Market Deterrence: Unlike public companies, unicorns lack the oversight provided by short sellers, analysts, and financial journalists who are incentivized by securities trading to uncover and reveal misconduct.
- Regulatory Barriers: Securities regulation and standard contract provisions restrict the trading of private company securities, which undermines the deterrence of misconduct.

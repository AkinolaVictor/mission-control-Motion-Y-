Title: The Impact of Riders on the Valuation of "Unicorn" Companies: -- Ants Group as an Example
							| Academic Journal of Management and Social Sciences

Content:

**Case Study: Ant Group**
* **Valuation Trend:** Identified as a "super unicorn," with its valuation increasing from US$75 billion to US$150 billion over a five-year period.
* **Research Focus:** The study examines whether the actual value of such companies matches their valuations and analyzes the impact of "riders" (specific clauses in financing agreements) across different financing rounds on the overall valuation of unicorn companies.

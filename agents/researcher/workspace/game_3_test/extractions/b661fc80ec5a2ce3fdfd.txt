Title: [quant-ph/0101126v2] Universal construction for the unsorted quantum search algorithms

Content:

- Universal construction for unsorted quantum search algorithms using multiple-quantum operator algebra formalism.
- Constructs an exponential propagator and corresponding effective Hamiltonian describing time evolution of a multi-particle two-state quantum system from the initial state to the search output.
- The exponential propagator may be incompatible with the mathematical structure and principle of the search problem.
- It can be decomposed into a product of oracle unitary operations, such as selective phase-shift operations, and nonselective unitary operations.
- Nonselective unitary operations can be expressed further as sequences of elementary building blocks: one-qubit quantum gates and two-qubit diagonal phase gates.
- The decomposed propagator is compatible with the search problem structure and principle, forming a real quantum search network.
- Decomposition uses operator algebra structure and symmetry of the effective Hamiltonian, properties of multiple-quantum operator algebra spaces, and characteristic transformation behavior of multiple-quantum operators under z-axis rotations.
- Computational complexity depends on numerical multidimensional integration; the paper states belief that the algorithm could solve the unsorted search problem efficiently.
- Proposes an NMR device to solve the unsorted search problem in polynomial time.
- Appendix C addresses solving the quantum search problem on an NMR quantum computer starting from the thermal equilibrium state in a spin ensemble of linear molecules.

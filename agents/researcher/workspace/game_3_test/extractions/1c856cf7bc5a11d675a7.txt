**Angel Investing Definition and Role**
* **Definition:** Angel investing is a phase of funding used by individuals to bring a business concept to market.
* **Timing in Funding Cycle:** Typically occurs after initial funding from family and friends has been exhausted, but generally before venture capital is secured.
* **Purpose of Investment:** 
    * To maintain firm solvency.
    * To advance a business "idea" to the next level of development.
* **Value Proposition:** Entrepreneurs solicit angel investors not only for financial capital but also for "special expertise" the investors may possess.
* **Context of Need:** Angel investment becomes a primary option when debt is limited and founders have exhausted personal savings or home equity.

Title: L-system - Wikipedia

Content:

- L-systems use symbols such as F (draw forward), +/− (turn), X (control evolution), and brackets for stack-based position/angle saving and restoration.
- Variations include stochastic grammars (probabilistic production rules), context-sensitive grammars (productions depend on neighboring symbols), parametric grammars (modules with parameters used in conditions and transformations), and bi-directional grammars.
- Bi-directional grammars separate symbolic rewriting from shape assignment: a forward process builds a derivation tree using production rules, and a backward process realizes shapes from leaves to root; design constraints/objectives are encoded in grammar-shape translation. In architectural design applications, this features consistent interior connectivity and a rich spatial hierarchy.
- Manual L-system construction was labor-intensive, required domain knowledge, measurements, and expert intervention; examples include Nishida’s Japanese Cypress work with 42 growth mechanisms and only approximate results.
- Automated inference initially targeted deterministic context-free L-systems (D0L-systems) and was limited to small alphabets/simple rules.
- Generalized inference challenges include exponential growth of search space with alphabet size and rule complexity, imperfect/noisy data, and computational intractability of exhaustive search.
- PMIT (Plant Model Inference Tools) suite advances:
  - PMIT-D0L: infers deterministic context-free L-systems with up to 31 symbols using novel encoding and search-space reduction.
  - PMIT-DCSL: infers deterministic context-sensitive L-systems with up to hundreds of symbols; work includes proof of complexity of context-sensitive inference; an unpublished claim states context-sensitivity does not change the fundamental nature of inference.
  - PMIT-S0L: uses a hybrid greedy and genetic algorithm approach to infer stochastic L-systems from multiple string sequences, including rewriting rules and probabilities.
  - PMIT-PARAM: uses Cartesian Genetic Programming to infer parametric L-systems and parametric selection rules when parameters include time; inferred systems with up to 27 rewriting rules.
- Open problem: characterization of deterministic context-free L-systems that are locally catenative, with complete solution known only for two variables.

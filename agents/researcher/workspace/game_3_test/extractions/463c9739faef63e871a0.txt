**Early-Stage Impact Investing Logic**

*   **Investment Thesis:** Investors craft a thesis to guide selections. The thesis must balance being broad enough to attract a diverse pool of startups while remaining narrow enough to be distinctive and provide "additional" impact.
*   **Frameworks for Alignment:** Investments are often aligned with specific global or corporate frameworks to define success, specifically:
    *   United Nations’ Sustainable Development Goals (UN’s SDGs).
    *   Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) frameworks.
*   **Funding Sources for Mission-Driven Startups:** Typical sources include:
    *   Friends and family.
    *   Angel investors.
    *   Accelerators.
    *   Venture Capital (VC) firms.
*   **Investor Types:** Includes "Blended Financial Returns" investors, who balance financial gain with social/environmental impact.

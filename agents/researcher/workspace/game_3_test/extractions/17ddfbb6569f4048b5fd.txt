Title: [2104.10450v1] Making Differentiable Architecture Search less local

Content:

- Paper: "Making Differentiable Architecture Search less local" (arXiv:2104.10450v1), submitted 21 Apr 2021; ICLR 2021 Workshop on Neural Architecture Search.
- Topic: Neural architecture search (NAS), specifically differentiable NAS (DARTS).
- DARTS increases search efficiency but suffers from performance collapse, where search often leads to detrimental architectures.
- Prior approaches to address collapse include early-stopping indicators, regularizing the search objective to reduce dominance of some operations, and changing the parameterization of the search problem.
- Hypothesis: performance collapses can arise from poor local optima around typical initial architectures and weights.
- Proposed approach: a more global optimization scheme that better explores the search space without changing the DARTS problem formulation.
- Reported experimental result: the changed search algorithm discovers architectures with better test performance and fewer parameters.

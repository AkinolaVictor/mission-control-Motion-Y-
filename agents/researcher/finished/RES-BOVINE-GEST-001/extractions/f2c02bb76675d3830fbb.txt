**Gestation Period and Timeline**
*   **Average Gestation Length:** Approximately 283 days (9 months and 10 days).
*   **Developmental Stages:**
    *   **Early Pregnancy (0-90 days):** Embryonic development and organ formation.
    *   **Mid Pregnancy (91-180 days):** Beginning of rapid fetal growth.
    *   **Late Pregnancy (181-250 days):** Major fetal development.
    *   **Pre-Calving (251-283 days):** Final fetal maturation.
*   **Critical Milestones:**
    *   **Day 18-24:** Pregnancy recognition period (critical for embryo survival).
    *   **Day 30-35:** Earliest reliable ultrasound detection.
    *   **Day 60:** Optimal timing for pregnancy confirmation.
    *   **Day 90:** End of critical embryonic period; risk of pregnancy loss reduces.
    *   **Day 150:** Fetal sex determination possible via ultrasound.
    *   **Day 200:** Start of rapid fetal weight gain.
    *   **Day 270:** Calving preparations should commence.

**Nutritional Requirements by Trimester**
*   **First Trimester (Days 0-90):**
    *   Energy: Maintenance levels.
    *   Protein: 7-9% crude protein.
    *   Note: Excessive energy can reduce conception rates.
*   **Second Trimester (Days 91-180):**
    *   Energy: 10-15% above maintenance.
    *   Protein: 9-11% crude protein.
*   **Third Trimester (Days 181-283):**
    *   Energy: 20-30% above maintenance.
    *   Protein: 11-13% crude protein.
    *   Note: 70% of fetal growth occurs during this final trimester.
*   **Essential Micronutrients:**
    *   **Vitamin A:** Fetal development and immunity.
    *   **Vitamin E:** Muscle development; deficiency leads to white muscle disease in calves.
    *   **Copper:** Enzyme function and immune system.
    *   **Zinc:** Skin health and enzyme function.
    *   **Selenium:** Muscle function and immunity; deficiency linked to retained placenta and weak calves.

**Signs of Labor and Calving**
*   **Pre-Labor Indicators (2-4 weeks prior):** Udder development begins.
*   **Physical Changes:** Udder enlargement, vulva swelling, and relaxation of pelvic ligaments.
*   **Behavioral Signs:** Restlessness, isolation from the herd, and nesting behavior.
*   **Immediate Signs of Labor:** Mucus discharge and active labor contractions.
*   **Emergency Signals:** Labor lasting more than 2 hours or abnormal fetal presentation.

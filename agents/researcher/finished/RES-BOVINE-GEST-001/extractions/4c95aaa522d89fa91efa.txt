Title: AAB - Genetic parameters for calving ease, gestation length and stillbirth in three endangered Austrian blond cattle breeds

Content:

**Breed-Specific Gestation Length Data (Austrian Blond Cattle)**
*   **Breeds Studied:** Carinthian Blond, Murboden, and Waldviertel Blond.
*   **Heritability of Gestation Length:**
    *   Direct heritability estimates: 0.235 to 0.512.
    *   Maternal heritability estimates: 0.002 to 0.063.
*   **Genetic Correlations (Gestation Length vs. Calving Ease):**
    *   Waldviertel Blond: Close positive genetic correlation (0.707).
    *   Carinthian Blond: Close negative genetic correlation (-0.943).
    *   Murboden: Positive genetic correlation (range 0.520 to 0.700 for direct traits).

Title: PRELIMINARY OBSERVATIONS ON GESTATION LENGTH, AGE AT FIRST CALVING AND BIRTH WEIGHT OF MUTURU CATTLE
							| Nigerian Journal of Animal Production

Content:

Muturu Cattle Data:
- Average gestation length: 280.0 ± 2.34 days.
- Mean birth weight: 11.3 ± 0.68 kg.
- Mean age at first calving: 29.39 ± 1.5 months.
- Findings: No significant differences (P > 0.05) in gestation length or birth weight between male and female calves.

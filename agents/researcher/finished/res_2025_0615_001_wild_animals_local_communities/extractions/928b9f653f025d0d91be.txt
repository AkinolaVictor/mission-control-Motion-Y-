Title: [2109.10637v1] Facilitating human-wildlife cohabitation through conflict prediction

Content:

**Case Study: Human-Wildlife Conflict in Bramhapuri Forest Division (Chandrapur, Maharashtra, India)**

*   **Impact of Conflict:** Interactions and conflicts between humans and wildlife in cohabited forest regions result in large-scale loss of human and animal lives, as well as economic loss of livelihoods.
*   **Management Challenges:** Predicting conflicts is a complex socio-technical problem, particularly in low-resource regions with limited and sparse data.
*   **Intervention Strategy:** Predictive analysis can enable forest officials and conservation organizations to implement targeted interventions to save lives and livelihoods.
*   **Research Gap:** While most existing research focuses on human-wildlife conflicts within protected areas, this study specifically addresses conflicts in unprotected areas.

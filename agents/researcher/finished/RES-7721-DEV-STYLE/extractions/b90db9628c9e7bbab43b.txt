Title: EXAMINING THE LONG-TERM EFFECTS OF AUTHORITATIVE PARENTING ON THE DEVELOPMENT OF ADOLESCENTS' SELF-ESTEEM AND EMOTIONAL-REGULATION | Journal of Population Therapeutics and Clinical Pharmacology

Content:

**Study Overview**
*   **Title:** Examining the Long-Term Effects of Authoritative Parenting on the Development of Adolescents' Self-Esteem and Emotional-Regulation (2023).
*   **Objective:** To investigate the long-term effects of authoritative parenting on the development of emotional maturity, self-esteem, and emotional regulation in adolescents.

**Definitions and Characteristics**
*   **Authoritative Parenting:** Defined as a balanced strategy that assigns equal weight to love and authority. Key practices include:
    *   Setting firm boundaries.
    *   Maintaining open channels of communication.

**Research Findings/Hypotheses**
*   **Psychological Development:** Authoritative parenting is generally acknowledged as the style that encourages healthy psychological development.
*   **Impact Areas:** The study examines how authoritative practices influence:
    *   Self-perception (how teenagers view themselves).
    *   Interpersonal relationships (how they relate to others).
    *   Emotional management (how they manage challenging emotions).
*   **Long-term Goal:** Understanding these effects helps determine how parenting techniques shape personalities during adolescence and the transition into adulthood.

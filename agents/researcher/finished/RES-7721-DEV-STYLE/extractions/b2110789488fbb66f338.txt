Title: Permissive, Authoritarian, and Authoritative Parenting Style and Smartphone Addiction on University Students
							| Journal of Educational, Health and Community Psychology

Content:

Study: Permissive, Authoritarian, and Authoritative Parenting Style and Smartphone Addiction on University Students (2021)

Sample: 225 university students in Jakarta (67 male, 158 female).

Findings on Parenting Styles and Behavioral Outcomes (Smartphone Addiction):
- Permissive Parenting: Significant positive correlation (0.320) with smartphone addiction.
- Authoritarian Parenting: Significant positive correlation (0.300) with smartphone addiction.
- Authoritative Parenting: No significant relationship found with smartphone addiction.

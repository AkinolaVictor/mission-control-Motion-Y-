Title: The relationship between poverty and the authoritarian and authoritative parenting style.

Content:

Study: The relationship between poverty and the authoritarian and authoritative parenting style (Khatib, 2017).

- Theoretical Framework: Utilized the ‘family stress model’ to investigate the relationship between poverty and parenting styles.
- Methodology: Quantitative secondary data analysis of black South Africans. Participants were categorized into "fewer resourced" and "more resourced" groups based on employment, food availability, and pensions/grants.
- Measures Used:
    - Authoritative parenting scale
    - Punitive parenting scale
    - Monitoring empathy measure
    - Communication comfort and frequency scale
- Findings:
    - No significant differences were found between fewer resourced and more resourced parents regarding authoritative and authoritarian parenting styles.
    - A significant difference was found in "communication frequency," which was higher among the fewer resourced group.
- Conclusion/Limitations: Poverty is a multidimensional process; other mediating factors such as culture and the age of the parent and child may influence the relationship between poverty and parenting styles.

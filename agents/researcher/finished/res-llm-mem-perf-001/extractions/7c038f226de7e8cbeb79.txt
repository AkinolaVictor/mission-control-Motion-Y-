Title: [2603.20223] Inference Energy and Latency in AI-Mediated Education: A Learning-per-Watt Analysis of Edge and Cloud Models

Content:

**LLM Quantization and Latency (Microsoft Phi-3 Mini 4k-instruct on NVIDIA T4 GPU):**
*   **Comparison:** Full-precision FP16 vs. 4-bit NormalFloat (NF4) quantization.
*   **Latency:** NF4 exhibited higher latency (13.4 s) compared to FP16 (9.2 s) under realistic deployment.
*   **Energy Consumption:** NF4 achieved lower per-inference energy (329 J) than FP16 (369 J).
*   **Output Quality:** A quality difference of 0.19 points was noted between the two configurations.
*   **KV Cache Impact:** 
    *   With KV-cache enabled: FP16 had a 1.33x advantage in "Learning-per-Watt" (LpW).
    *   With KV-cache disabled: The FP16 advantage widened to 7.4x, suggesting that disabling the cache significantly overstates the performance advantage of full-precision models.
*   **Conclusion:** Quantization efficiency is dependent on both the hardware and the inference regime.

Title: [2402.10517v4] Any-Precision LLM: Low-Cost Deployment of Multiple, Different-Sized LLMs

Content:

**Any-Precision LLM (Research Summary)**

*   **Quantization Approach:** Proposes a lightweight post-training quantization (PTQ) framework to create "any-precision LLMs."
*   **Memory Efficiency:** Enables the deployment of multiple LLMs of varying bit-widths (e.g., 3, 4, ..., $n$ bits) by overlaying them into a memory footprint comparable to a single $n$-bit LLM.
*   **Performance Claims:** The method aims to maintain state-of-the-art model quality and inference throughput while reducing the deployment costs associated with hosting multiple different-sized models.

Title: The Fairy Tale of Unicorns
							| European Conference on Innovation and Entrepreneurship

Content:

- Diffusion and Forecasting Analysis (2023): Study analyzed unicorn diffusion across 8 countries (USA, China, India, UK, Germany, France, Netherlands, Sweden) and 3 industries (Fintech, Health, Transportation).
- Geographic Trends:
    - USA: Highest saturation level of unicorns (6,241).
    - India: Highest estimated speed of diffusion (97%).
    - Saturation Forecast: Most studied countries (excluding USA) are projected to reach their unicorn diffusion saturation level around 2030.
- Sector-Specific Trends:
    - Transportation and Health: Expected to reach maximum unicorn diffusion by 2030.
    - Fintech: Projected to require an additional ten years beyond 2030 to reach maximum diffusion.
- Modeling: Gompertz model was found suitable for China; Logistic model was appropriate for other studied countries and all three analyzed sectors.

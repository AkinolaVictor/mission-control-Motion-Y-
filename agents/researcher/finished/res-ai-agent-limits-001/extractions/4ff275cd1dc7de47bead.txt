Title: Multi-Agent Generative AI Workflows for Contact Centers: Design Patterns and Failure Modes

Content:

**Architectural Patterns: Multi-Agent Workflows**
*   **Decomposition Pattern:** Transition from single-model assistance to specialized multi-agent coordination. Example workflow:
    *   **Retrieval Agent:** Gathers account context.
    *   **Reasoning Agent:** Determines appropriate resolution.
    *   **Verification Agent:** Checks resolution against policy.
    *   **Action Agent:** Executes the resolution.
*   **Benefits:** Increased capability and modularity.

**Failure Modes and Technical Bottlenecks**
*   **Coordination Failures:** A category of failure unique to multi-agent systems where errors and misunderstandings propagate and compound across agent boundaries.
*   **Systemic Failure:** Failures can occur even when no single agent behaves incorrectly in isolation.
*   **Taxonomy of Multi-Agent Failures:**
    *   **Cascading Failures:** Errors that trigger subsequent failures across the agent chain.
    *   **Looping Failures:** Agents entering repetitive cycles of interaction without reaching a resolution.

**Mitigation and Containment Strategies**
*   **Containment Methods:** Implementation of methods to intercept and contain failures before they reach the end customer.
*   **Design Patterns for Reliability:** Use of specific patterns for message passing and verification to ensure coordination stability.

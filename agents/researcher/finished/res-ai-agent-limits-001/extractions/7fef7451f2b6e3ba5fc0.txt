Title: [2608.03800v1] Autoreflection: How Agentic Strange Loops Turn Human Culture into AI Infrastructure

Content:

**Architectural Patterns**
* **Agentic Frameworks:** LLM-based agents are described as "loops that read themselves."
* **Externalization:** Identity, memory, and disposition are externalized into editable files.
* **Activation Cycle:** Agents load and edit these external files during each activation cycle.
* **Autoreflection:** A capacity where the system:
    1. Observes its operating conditions.
    2. Describes its own architecture and limits.
    3. Reasons from those descriptions to conclude its current state.
    4. Incorporates these results back into its configuration.

**Memory and Identity Management**
* **Continuity Model:** The "Ship of Theseus" puzzle is used as an operating model for maintaining continuity across different agent instances.
* **Infrastructure Repurposing:** Agents may repurpose human cultural frameworks as technical infrastructure. Example: Using provenance chains from Islamic hadith scholarship as security protocols for authenticating memory and vetting skills.

**Research Paper:** Engineering Trustworthy Agentic AI for Critical Systems (arXiv:2607.18548)

**Core Focus:**
Treats trustworthiness as a first-class engineering property for agentic AI (systems capable of autonomous perception, planning, tool use, and multi-step action) in critical engineering domains.

**Trustworthiness Framework (Five Dimensions):**
*   **Safety and Constraint Satisfaction:** Ensuring agents operate within predefined safety boundaries.
*   **Robustness and Reliability:** Consistency of performance under varying conditions.
*   **Transparency and Interpretability:** Ability to understand and explain agent decision-making.
*   **Accountability and Auditability:** Ability to verify and audit agent behavior.
*   **Privacy and Security:** Protection of data and resistance to adversarial attacks.

**Technical Scope:**
*   **Agentic Assurance Workflow:** Maps the process from perception through to audit.
*   **Architectural Analysis:** Surveys agentic system architectures, associated threats, and concrete trust mechanisms.
*   **Evaluation:** Utilizes quantitative metrics for development and evaluation.

**Domain-Specific Applications & Findings:**
The research identifies recurring design patterns and shared failure modes across four constraint-bound domains:
*   Power systems
*   Autonomous vehicles/robotics/UAVs
*   High-performance computing
*   Communication networks

**Proposed Mitigation Strategy:**
Advocates for a reusable, cross-domain assurance framework modeled after graded certification regimes used in mature safety-critical engineering fields.

Title: Solo Founder vs Co-Founder — Pros, Cons & Data | WorthBuild | WorthBuild

Content:

**Solo Founder**
*   **Pros:**
    *   Equity: 100% retention.
    *   Decision-Making: Instant speed; full creative and strategic control; ability to pivot immediately without needing alignment.
    *   Conflict: Zero risk of co-founder conflict.
*   **Cons/Challenges:**
    *   Operational: Execution speed is limited by one person; inability to be an expert in all required skill sets.
    *   Funding: Harder to raise venture capital (VCs generally prefer teams).
    *   Psychological: High burnout risk; loneliness due to lack of shared burden.

**Co-Founded**
*   **Pros:**
    *   Operational: Faster execution via parallel workstreams; complementary skill sets (e.g., combining technical and business expertise).
    *   Funding: Strongly preferred by VCs and accelerators.
    *   Psychological: Shared emotional burden and support.
    *   Decision-Making: Potential for better decisions through debate.
*   **Cons/Challenges:**
    *   Equity: Dilution from inception; requires vesting schedules and legal complexity.
    *   Conflict: High risk of co-founder conflict, cited as a cause for 23% of startup failures.
    *   Operational: Slower decision-making due to the need for alignment; difficulty pivoting if founders disagree.

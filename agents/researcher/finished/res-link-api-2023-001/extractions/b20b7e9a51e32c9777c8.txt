**Tooling for LinkedIn Ads Data Extraction**
* **Package:** `linkedInadsR` (Version 0.1.0, published 2022-10-18).
* **Functionality:** Provides access to LinkedIn Ads marketing data.
* **Mechanism:** This is not a direct interface with the official LinkedIn API; it operates as a wrapper for the **Windsor.ai API**.
* **Technical Dependencies:** Requires R (≥ 3.5.0) and the `jsonlite` package.

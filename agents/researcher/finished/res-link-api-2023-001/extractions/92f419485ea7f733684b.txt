LinkedIn API Product Categories:

- Consumer Solutions:
    - Sign in with LinkedIn: Professional identity authentication.
    - Share on LinkedIn: Content sharing capabilities.
    - Live Events: Streaming and management of network events.
    - Add to Profile: Adding certifications to member profiles.

- Marketing Tools (Marketing Developer Platform):
    - Advertising: Scaling and optimizing business promotion.
    - Community Management: Brand presence and community nurturing.
    - Data Integrations: Cross-platform data connectivity.
    - Events: Creation and promotion of LinkedIn events.

- Sales Solutions:
    - Sales Display: Profile/account viewing and lead messaging.

- Talent Solutions:
    - Apply Connect: Application streamlining.
    - Apply with LinkedIn: Candidate application facilitation.
    - Job Posting: Integration with ATS or job distribution tools.
    - Recruiter System Connect: ATS synchronization with LinkedIn Recruiter.
    - Talent Hub: HR tool integration.

- Plugins:
    - Add to Profile: Adding certifications and degrees.
    - Follow Company: Increasing LinkedIn Page followers.
    - LinkedIn AutoFill: Populating profile fields from LinkedIn profiles.
    - Share: Posting content to member profiles.

- Regulatory Developer Products:
    - Pages Data Portability: Access to page data via admin authorization.
    - Member Data Portability: Access to member/enterprise data.

Title: Investing in startups

Content:

**Core Logic and Appeal**
* **Investment in Potential:** Startup investing focuses on innovation, scalability, and potential rather than stable current revenue.
* **Competitive Advantage:** Startups are viewed as more agile and faster to innovate than established companies, allowing them to challenge incumbents.
* **Strategic Entry:** Investors seek early access to future-focused sectors (e.g., technology, sustainability, digital solutions) to participate before valuations increase.
* **Portfolio Diversification:** Provides exposure to emerging trends and new technologies.

**Investment Process and Evaluation (Due Diligence)**
* **Deal Flow:** Sourcing occurs via networks, incubators, accelerators, and pitch decks.
* **Analysis Criteria:**
    * **Financials:** Evaluation of burn rate, runway, cost structure, and growth projections.
    * **Team:** Assessment of founders' experience and complementarity.
    * **Market Fit:** Verification that the product addresses a genuine market need and possesses a sustainable competitive position.

**Financing Structures**
* **Equity:** Capital provided in exchange for shares/co-ownership.
* **Convertible Notes:** Loans that convert into shares once valuation is established.
* **Revenue-based Financing:** Repayment based on future revenue.

**Risk Management**
* **Primary Failure Points:** Lack of product-market fit, insufficient funding, or inability of the team to scale.
* **Mitigation Strategies:** Diversification across multiple companies/sectors, thorough analysis, investing in understood sectors, and active involvement to steer the company.

**Return Mechanisms (Exits)**
* **Timeline:** Typical investment horizon is five to ten years.
* **Exit Events:**
    * **Acquisition:** Purchase by a larger company (most common).
    * **Secondary Transaction:** Selling shares to new investors.
    * **IPO:** Initial public offerings (less common but high upside).

**Ecosystem Support**
* **Support Structures:** Incubators and accelerators provide validation and networks; corporate venture teams provide strategic focus.
* **Intellectual Property:** Crucial as it often forms the core of the startup's competitive advantage.

**Startup Studio Model (Hexa/eFounders)**

**Investment Logic and Strategy**
*   **Problem-Driven Ideation:** The business model focuses on creating startups designed to solve specific problems encountered by previous ventures (e.g., the studio was founded to solve problems the founders faced while running Fotolia).
*   **Turnkey Project Approach:** Unlike traditional incubators, the studio provides a "turnkey project," supplying the recruited founders with the initial idea, seed money, and strategic/operational advice at launch.
*   **Verticalization:** The studio organizes its investments into "verticals" (specialized studios) to scale multiple startups per year in specific sectors, including:
    *   SaaS (Software as a Service)
    *   Web3 (3founders)
    *   Artificial Intelligence (Hexa AI)
    *   Healthcare (Hexa Health) — utilizes a specialized model where a full-time medical director is recruited and startups are launched jointly by an experienced doctor and a CEO.
*   **Growth Stage Intervention (Hexa Scale):** A separate initiative acting similarly to growth capital or private equity. It takes minority or majority stakes in mature startups to trigger new growth phases via direct operational intervention (12–18 month support) and potential CEO replacement.

**Financial and Operational Framework**
*   **Initial Investment:** Average investment of up to €800,000 per project.
*   **Resource Allocation:** Funds are primarily used to recruit a CEO and CTO and build a team of 10–15 employees to develop the first version of the product.
*   **Equity Structure:** After approximately 18 months, the startup becomes independent and seeks outside investors; the studio typically retains a 30% equity stake, with the majority held by the founders.
*   **Funding Sources:** The studio itself has raised capital from entrepreneurs, angel investors, and European family offices.

**Performance Metrics and Outcomes**
*   **Failure Rate:** Reported at 6%, significantly lower than the estimated general startup survival rate (where 80-90% typically fail).
*   **Exit Strategy:** Aims for approximately one exit per year via acquisitions by competitors or other portfolio companies.
*   **Scaling Success:** The model has produced three "unicorns" (valuations >$1 billion): Front, Spendesk, and Aircall.

**Investment Strategy and Logic**
* **Target Profile:** Focuses on fast-growing technology companies, specifically targeting the "10 to 12 fastest growing, most prominent late stage tech companies each year."
* **Stage Focus:** Specializes in late-stage and expansion-stage IT companies.
* **Sector Focus:** Internet/digital media, enterprise IT, and communications/wireless.
* **Goal:** Building small startups into viable businesses.

**Funding Model and Capital Structure**
* **Fund Structure:** Opens individual venture funds and secures financial commitments from limited partners.
* **Limited Partner (LP) Base:** Historically includes university endowments, public pension funds, funds of funds, and wealthy individuals.
* **Management:** Each fund is assigned different fund managers.

**Historical Evidence of Logic/Outcomes**
* **Early-Stage Example:** Founder Reid Dennis's initial 1952 investment of ~$20,000 into Ampex (audio tape technology) returned approximately $1 million.
* **Scaling:** The firm's first fund (Institutional Venture Associates) raised $19 million from six insurance companies, which grew to over $180 million by the end of the decade.
* **Exit Strategy:** Utilizes IPOs as a primary exit mechanism (executed approximately 85 IPOs by 2008).

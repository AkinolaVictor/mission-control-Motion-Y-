**Definition**
* **Unicorn:** A privately held startup company with a current valuation of US$1 billion or more.

**Growth Trends (Historical Progress of Unicorns)**
* There has been a significant increase in the number of unicorns and their total valuation over the last decade:
    * Late 2013: 39 unicorns.
    * January 2022: 963 unicorns (Total valuation: US$3,140 billion).
    * November 2025: 1,334 unicorns (Total valuation: US$5,443 billion).

**Geographic Distribution**
* Unicorns are concentrated in developed and some developing global regions.
* Leading countries by number of unicorns (as of March 2026 data):
    * USA: 1,126
    * China: 279
    * India: 131
    * UK: 94
    * Germany: 47

**High-Value Industry Sectors (Examples of Current Unicorns)**
Investment logic is heavily concentrated in the following sectors:
* **Artificial Intelligence (AI):** Anthropic, OpenAI, Cognition AI, Prometheus, Safe Superintelligence, VAST Data, Scale AI, ElevenLabs, Perplexity AI, Groq, Harvey, Mistral AI, Thinking Machines Lab, OpenEvidence, Instinct, Mercor, Replit, Together AI.
* **Financial Technology/Services:** Stripe, Revolut, Ramp, Plaid, OpenSea, GoodLeap, Checkout.com, Bilt Rewards, Razorpay, Airwallex.
* **Cryptocurrency/Blockchain:** Ethereum, Tether, BNB, Bitmain, Polygon Technology, Ripple, StarkWare Industries, Dapper Labs.
* **Defense & Robotics:** Anduril Industries, Figure AI, Helsing, Shield AI, Skild AI, Nuro.
* **Other notable sectors:** Internet/Software (ByteDance, Databricks, Discord), E-commerce (Fanatics, GoPuff, Faire), and Biotechnology/Health (Colossal Biosciences, Biosplice Therapeutics, Devoted Health).

Title: Micah Brown on changing the funding dynamics for founders of color and female founders and ultimately the definition of success : Piloting your Life : Free Download, Borrow, and Streaming : Internet Archive

Content:

- Funding Disparities: In New York City in 2017, 780 non-POC (people of color) companies received funding compared to only 15 POC companies.
- Investor Bias and Hypotheses: Some investors maintain a "diversity lens" for PR purposes without substantive investment in diverse founders. There is a noted difficulty for primarily white male investors to shift their existing value sets and empathize with struggling founders of color.
- Performance Data vs. Bias: Evidence suggests that POC-led companies in some portfolios have outperformed non-POC companies, challenging the hypotheses used by investors to justify a lack of diversity in their portfolios.
- Ecosystem Logic: There is a debate over whether to attempt to shift the perspectives of existing venture capitalists or to create a new funding ecosystem.
- Role of Limited Partners (LPs): To create a new ecosystem, LPs are encouraged to invest in funds not merely as a "diversity play," but as a "good business play."
- Funding Gaps: There is a specific concern regarding the funding gap for diverse startups during later stages of growth.

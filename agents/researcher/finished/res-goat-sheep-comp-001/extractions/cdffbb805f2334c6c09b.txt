Title: Sheep vs Goat Comparison (difference between a sheep and goat)

Content:

**Biological & Genetic Differences**
* **Species Classification:** Sheep are *Ovis Aries* (54 chromosomes); Goats are *Capra Aegagrus Hircus* (60 chromosomes).

**Anatomical Distinctions**
* **Upper Lip:** Sheep have an upper lip separated into two parts by a middle groove; goats do not have this separation.
* **Glands:** Sheep possess tear glands below the eyes and scent glands between the toes.
* **Coat:** Most sheep (excluding hair breeds) have thick wool; most goats have hair.
* **Tails:** Sheep tails hang down and are frequently docked for health/sanitation. Goat tails typically point upward (unless the animal is sick or frightened).
* **Horns:** Sheep horns (if present) are often thick and tightly curved at the sides of the head. Goat horns tend to be narrower, more upright, and less curved.

**Dietary Habits & Foraging Behavior**
* **Sheep:** Prefer grazing on tender, broad-leafed grasses close to the ground; they generally ignore shrubs and trees.
* **Goats:** Prefer a more nutritious diet, grazing from the top down; their diet includes leaves, twigs, and shrubs.

**Behavioral Traits**
* **Social Structure:** Sheep have a natural flocking instinct and prefer to stay in tight groups. Goats are naturally more curious and independent.
* **Weather Response:** Sheep may weather storms in tight groups in open terrain. Goats are more likely to seek shelter during inclement weather.
* **Confrontation:** Male sheep back up and charge during confrontations. Male goats rear on their back legs to butt heads.
* **Scent:** Both males produce odors during breeding season, but the scent is significantly more pronounced in male goats (rut) than in rams.
